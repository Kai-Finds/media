Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · Applies to the Kai Finds Facebook Page, Threads and Instagram accounts (@kaifinds.ai, @thekaifinds), the Kai Finds Discord server and the "PostAutomation" Meta app.

Who we are

Kai Finds is a small media brand about AI tools for founders, makers and developers. Its posts are written with the help of AI and every post and reply is reviewed and approved by a human before publication. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL.

What the app does

The PostAutomation app is used only by Kai Finds to manage its own accounts. It publishes our own posts to our Facebook Page and Threads profile, reads the comments and replies on our own posts, reads mentions of @kaifinds.ai, reads public posts from a short list of public creator accounts and public keyword search results about AI tools, and reads insights (views, likes, replies) of our own posts. It never publishes on behalf of anyone else and is not offered to other users.

Data we process

  • Public content: the text, author username, timestamp and link of public Threads posts, replies and mentions related to our posts or to AI tools.
  • Our own account data: our posts, their insights and our follower counts.
  • We do not collect private messages, email addresses, phone numbers, location, payment data or any data of people who simply view our posts.

Why we process it

  • To answer comments and questions on our posts.
  • To find public conversations about AI tools where a useful reply can help, and to draft that reply for human approval.
  • To measure which of our own posts are useful to our audience (aggregated statistics only).

We do not sell, rent or share this data, do not use it for advertising profiles and do not use it to train AI models. Drafts are generated by a language model on our server; only the public text needed to draft a reply is sent to it.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a private database on our server, accessible only to the Kai Finds team. Public posts collected for reply drafting are deleted after 30 days; for replies we actually sent, we keep only the link and our own reply text. Aggregated statistics about our own posts are kept for up to 24 months. Access tokens are kept in a protected file on that server, readable only by the publishing process, and are never shared.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to access or delete any data we hold about your public posts or username, or to stop replying to your posts.

How to request deletion: send an email to CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Data deletion request" and your Threads or Facebook username, or send a direct message to @kaifinds.ai on Threads or Instagram. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by reply. If you remove the app's access from your Meta account settings, we also delete any data linked to that account.

Children

Our content targets adults working with technology. We do not knowingly process data from children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change, with a new "last updated" date.
